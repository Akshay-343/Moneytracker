Everything your money needs

Works with any currency, installs on your phone like a native app, and comes in light and dark.

Transactions

Income and expenses with categories, accounts, tags and notes. Search, filter, CSV import and export, receipt attachments.

Dashboard

Spent, income, net savings and daily average, with income vs expense and category breakdown charts for any date range.

Subscriptions

Monthly and yearly cost roll-up, renewal countdowns, reminders before billing and optional auto-posting of each charge.

Budgets

Per-category and overall monthly budgets with progress bars and alerts as you approach the limit.

Savings goals

Targets with deadlines, and the exact monthly amount you need to put aside to reach them on time.

AI tips & Ask Pursely

Personalised saving tips and a question box powered by Groq. The model only ever sees an aggregated summary of your data.

Insights & PDF report

Month-end projection, savings rate, month-over-month category changes, top merchants and a downloadable PDF report.

Accounts & transfers

Bank, cash and card accounts with live balances, and transfers between them that keep both sides in sync.

Secure sign-in

Email and password with JWT access and refresh tokens, Sign in with Google, password reset by email and rate limiting.

How it's built

A React PWA talking to a FastAPI backend over a versioned REST API, with PostgreSQL and background jobs for the automation.

1

Money is exact

Amounts are NUMERIC(12,2) in the database and Decimal in Python. Never floats.

2

Multi-tenant by construction

Every query filters by the current user, and tests assert one user can never read another's data.

3

Safe uploads

Receipts are checked against an allow-list and their magic bytes, size-capped and stored under random names.

4

Idempotent jobs

Recurring transactions and reminders can run any number of times without duplicates, thanks to dedupe keys.

5

Token revocation

A per-user token version invalidates every outstanding JWT after a password reset.

6

Verified migrations

CI migrates up, down and up again on PostgreSQL, and fails if models drift from migrations.
